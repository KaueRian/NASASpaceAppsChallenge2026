**EarthTrend Detective** helps scientists, agronomists, educators, journalists, and citizens separate **statistically verified multi-decadal climate shifts** from short-term weather noise anywhere on Earth. Styled in a **Dark Editorial Forensic Interface** (paired with archival high-contrast shareable Evidence Cards), it ingests 45 years (`1981–2025`) of open **NASA POWER (MERRA-2)** climatology and **NASA GIBS (MODIS)** satellite imagery to answer four forensic questions for any coordinate or municipality: **What** is changing, **Where** is it changing, **By how much**, and **Is the evidence statistically significant?**

> **Observational Statistical Audit — Without Single-Cause Physical Attribution:**  
> Statistical verdicts generated by **EarthTrend Detective** evaluate the monotonic direction, rate of change, and significance of gridded satellite/reanalysis time series (`0.5° × 0.625°`). A confirmed **Mann-Kendall** verdict ($p < 0.01$ or $p < 0.05$) and **Theil-Sen** 95% confidence interval demonstrate that an observed 45-year trajectory is unlikely to arise from random interannual variability; they **do not** by themselves establish single-cause physical attribution (e.g., global radiative forcing vs. regional deforestation vs. ENSO/AMO cycles) and **do not** replace local meteorological station calibration or field agronomic trials. See our full [**Scientific Audit (`docs/science/AUDIT.md`)**](docs/science/AUDIT.md) for mathematical equations, DOIs, data hygiene rules, and field validation protocols.

## 🔬 Scientific Interpretation & Statistical Engine

Unlike conventional climate dashboards that plot raw Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) lines—which are easily skewed by single-year El Niño or La Niña extremes—**EarthTrend Detective** runs a 6-stage forensic pipeline in [`src/lib/stats/`](src/lib/stats/):

1. **NASA Data Hygiene & Hydrological Seasonality ([`aggregate.ts`](src/lib/nasa/aggregate.ts)):**
   - Strips NASA POWER `-999` fill sentinels and requires **$\ge 80\%$ valid months** per seasonal window (10/12 months for `Annual`; 5/6 months for `Oct–Mar` and `Apr–Sep`).
   - Preserves the **Southern Hemisphere hydrological wet season (`Oct–Mar`)** by pairing Oct–Dec of year $Y-1$ with Jan–Mar of year $Y$.
   - Converts daily precipitation rates ($\text{mm/day}$) into exact calendar-month totals ($\text{mm}$, accounting for leap years) and dimensionless soil wetness (`0–1`) into percentage points (`0–100%`).
2. **Non-Parametric Mann-Kendall Trend Test ([`mannKendall.ts`](src/lib/stats/mannKendall.ts)):**
   - Computes rank concordance $S$, tie-corrected variance $\operatorname{Var}(S)$, continuity-corrected $Z$-score, and two-sided $p$-value ([Mann 1945](https://doi.org/10.2307/1907187); [Kendall 1975](https://archive.org/details/rankcorrelationm0000kend)).
3. **Theil-Sen Robust Slope Estimator + 95% Confidence Interval ([`sensSlope.ts`](src/lib/stats/sensSlope.ts)):**
   - Calculates the median of all $N = n(n-1)/2$ pairwise slopes ($\text{yr}^{-1}$ and $\text{decade}^{-1}$) plus analytical **95% confidence bounds** ([Sen 1968](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1968.10480934); [Gilbert 1987](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/7037501)).
4. **OLS Diagnostic Comparison & Lag-1 Serial Autocorrelation ([`regression.ts`](src/lib/stats/regression.ts)):**
   - Computes parametric OLS slope, $R^2$, and lag-1 serial correlation $r_1$. Automatically triggers `warnings.highAutocorrelation = true` whenever $\|r_1\| > 0.30$ and `warnings.shortRecord = true` when $n < 15$.
5. **Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate Control ([`benjaminiHochberg.ts`](src/lib/stats/benjaminiHochberg.ts)):**
   - Adjusts $p$-values into FDR $q$-values when screening multiple variable-season combinations ([Benjamini & Hochberg 1995](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x)).
6. **Standardized Climatological Anomalies ([`analyzeTrend.ts`](src/lib/stats/analyzeTrend.ts)):**
   - Flags extreme years ($\|z_i\| > 1.5$) and drives the *Show Your Stripes* visualization ([`<ClimateStripes />`](src/components/share/ClimateStripes.tsx)).

### 3-Tier Offline-First Resilience

Designed so judges and field users never hit a blank screen—even under hackathon Wi-Fi congestion or external API rate limits:

1. **Tier 1 — Instant Zero-Latency Presets & Browser Cache ([`clientInvestigate.ts`](src/lib/nasa/clientInvestigate.ts)):**
   - The 4 featured dossiers (`src/data/presets/case-01..04.json`) are pre-computed and bundled with the app for **0 ms offline loading**. Custom live queries are cached in both an in-memory `Map` and browser `localStorage`.
2. **Tier 2 — Cloudflare Workers Edge API + D1 SQLite Cache ([`src/worker/index.ts`](src/worker/index.ts)):**
   - `GET /api/investigate` rounds coordinates to `0.5°` (matching MERRA-2's native grid) and caches raw NASA POWER payloads in **Cloudflare D1 (`earthtrend-db`)** via auto-initialized schema ([`dbSchema.ts`](src/worker/dbSchema.ts)).
   - `POST /api/dossiers`, `GET /api/dossiers/:id`, and `GET /api/dossiers` store structured, non-free-text investigation parameters for permanent shareable URLs (`/dossier/:id`).
3. **Tier 3 — Direct Client-to-NASA Fallback:**
   - If the Worker endpoint is unreachable in a static preview environment, `runClientInvestigation()` automatically fetches directly from `https://power.larc.nasa.gov` and executes the TypeScript statistical engine in the browser.
