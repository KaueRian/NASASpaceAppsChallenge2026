**GeoTrend** helps scientists, agronomists, educators, journalists, and citizens separate **statistically verified multi-decadal climate shifts** from short-term weather noise anywhere on Earth. Styled in a **Dark Editorial Forensic Interface** (paired with high-contrast archival Evidence Cards, custom SVG/PNG branding in [`public/brand/`](public/brand/), bilingual **`EN | PT-BR`** UI, and **`°C | °F`** unit switching), it ingests 45 years (`1981–2025`) of open **NASA POWER (MERRA-2)** climatology and **NASA GIBS (MODIS)** satellite imagery to answer four forensic questions for any coordinate or municipality: **What** is changing, **Where** is it changing, **By how much**, and **Is the evidence statistically significant?**

> **Observational Statistical Audit — Without Single-Cause Physical Attribution:**  
> Statistical verdicts generated by **GeoTrend** evaluate the monotonic direction, rate of change, and significance of gridded satellite/reanalysis time series (`0.5° × 0.625°`). A confirmed **Mann-Kendall** verdict ($p < 0.01$ or $p < 0.05$) and **Theil-Sen** 95% confidence interval demonstrate that an observed 45-year trajectory is unlikely to arise from random interannual variability; they **do not** by themselves establish single-cause physical attribution (e.g., global radiative forcing vs. regional deforestation vs. ENSO/AMO cycles) and **do not** replace local meteorological station calibration or field agronomic trials. See our full [**Scientific Audit (`docs/science/AUDIT.md`)**](docs/science/AUDIT.md) for mathematical equations, DOIs, data hygiene rules, and field validation protocols.

## 🎯 The Signature Moment (30-Second Walkthrough)

Every investigation follows an auditable, zero-login forensic pipeline:

**Ask a Natural-Language Climate Question in PT or EN (or Pick from the 20-Case Paginated Catalog / Click Any Map Coordinate) ➔ Watch the Transparent 3-Stage Pipeline (`AI Filters` → `NASA POWER + Mann-Kendall + BH FDR` → `AI Synthesis`) ➔ Inspect the 45-Year Time Series (`1981–2025`), Seasonal Bars & *Show Your Stripes* Anomaly Barcode ➔ Audit the Non-Parametric Verdict (Mann-Kendall $S/Z/p$, Theil-Sen Slope with 95% CI, OLS $R^2$, Lag-1 Autocorrelation Warning & 4-Variable FDR Scan) ➔ Compare Side-by-Side NASA GIBS / MODIS "Before vs. After" (`2000` vs. `2025`) Imagery or Export a Shareable Satellite Mosaic ➔ Download Evidence Cards (`Story 9:16`, `Square 1:1`, `Slide 16:9`, or `Paper Figure` PNG with QR Code), Time-Series `CSV`, Academic Citations (`Summary`, `ABNT`, `APA`, `BibTeX`), or a Permanent Share Link (`/dossier/:id`) — or Open `/explore` for a Birth-Date Climate Timeline.**

## ✨ Platform Capabilities

- **3-Stage AI Climate Copilot + Deterministic Local Fallback:** Translates natural-language questions into validated NASA POWER filters (`POST /api/ai/translate`), executes real statistical analysis (`Stage 2`), and generates physical-context syntheses with explicit `Correlation ≠ Causation` safeguards (`POST /api/ai/interpret`), using a best-first adaptive Gemini model & thinking-effort cascade (`geminiClient.ts`) backed by Cloudflare D1 cooldowns (`ai_model_cooldowns`), short fallback cache TTLs (`ai_cache`), and zero-key deterministic offline fallback (`fallback.ts`).
- **20 Curated Investigations & Paginated Catalog (`#cases`):** Ships `4` bundled zero-latency Featured Cases (`Case #01–04`) plus `16` regional, national, and global cases across Rondônia, the Amazon, Brazil, and international benchmarks (`src/data/investigations.ts`), filterable and paginated (`8` cards/page in UI + `GET /api/investigations?page=`), alongside paginated saved/shared dossiers (`5` items/page in `<RecentDossiersDrawer />` + `GET /api/dossiers?page=`).
- **Forensic Charts, Satellite Verification & Deep Links:** Interactive Line+Trend, Seasonal Bars, and *Show Your Stripes* visualizations (`°C | °F` aware), lazy-loaded Leaflet coordinate picker, NASA GIBS (`MODIS True Color` & `NDVI`) `Before vs. After` (`2000` vs. `2025`) comparator, year-by-year Timelapse (`satMode`/`satLayer`/`satYear` deep-links), and CORS-safe satellite PNG mosaic export (`satelliteExport.ts`).
- **4-Format Share Studio & Academic Citations:** Generates high-resolution HTML5 Canvas PNG exports for **Story 9:16** (`1080×1920`), **Square 1:1** (`1080×1080`), **Slide 16:9** (`1920×1080`), and high-contrast **Paper / Article** figures (`1600×1000`), raw `CSV` exports, and one-click **Summary**, **ABNT (NBR 6023)**, **APA**, and **BibTeX** citations (`dossierExport.ts`).
- **Explore Hub — Birth-Date Climate Timeline (`/explore`):** Scans all 4 NASA POWER variables across a user's birth month since `max(birthYear, 1981)` (`birthdayAnalysis.ts`), presenting a Wrapped-style visual story (`BirthdayCard.tsx`) backed by a plain-language *Detective Mode* FDR statistical breakdown.

## 🔬 Scientific Interpretation & Statistical Engine

Unlike conventional climate dashboards that plot raw Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) lines—which are easily skewed by single-year El Niño or La Niña extremes—**GeoTrend** runs a 6-stage forensic pipeline in [`src/lib/stats/`](src/lib/stats/):

1. **NASA Data Hygiene & Hydrological Seasonality ([`aggregate.ts`](src/lib/nasa/aggregate.ts)):**
   - Strips NASA POWER `-999` fill sentinels and requires **$\ge 80\%$ valid months** per seasonal window (10/12 months for `Annual`; 5/6 months for `Oct–Mar` and `Apr–Sep`).
   - Preserves the **Southern Hemisphere hydrological wet season (`Oct–Mar`)** by pairing Oct–Dec of year $Y-1$ with Jan–Mar of year $Y$.
   - Converts daily precipitation rates ($\text{mm/day}$) into exact calendar-month totals ($\text{mm}$, accounting for leap years) and dimensionless soil wetness (`0–1`) into percentage points (`0–100%`).
2. **Non-Parametric Mann-Kendall Trend Test ([`mannKendall.ts`](src/lib/stats/mannKendall.ts)):**
   - Computes rank concordance $S$, tie-corrected variance $\operatorname{Var}(S)$, continuity-corrected $Z$-score, and two-sided $p$-value ([Mann 1945](https://doi.org/10.2307/1907187); [Kendall 1975](https://archive.org/details/rankcorrelationm0000kend)).
3. **Theil-Sen Robust Slope Estimator + 95% Confidence Interval ([`sensSlope.ts`](src/lib/stats/sensSlope.ts)):**
   - Calculates the median of all $N = n(n-1)/2$ pairwise slopes ($\text{yr}^{-1}$ and $\text{decade}^{-1}$) plus analytical **95% confidence bounds** ([Sen 1968](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1968.10480934); [Gilbert 1987](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/7037501)).
4. **OLS Diagnostic Comparison & Lag-1 Serial Autocorrelation ([`regression.ts`](src/lib/stats/regression.ts)):**
   - Computes parametric OLS slope, $R^2$, and lag-1 serial correlation $r_1$. Automatically triggers `warnings.highAutocorrelation = true` whenever $\|r_1\| > 0.30$ and `warnings.shortRecord = true` when $n < 15$.
5. **Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate Control ([`benjaminiHochberg.ts`](src/lib/stats/benjaminiHochberg.ts)):**
   - Adjusts $p$-values into FDR $q$-values when screening multiple variable-season combinations ([Benjamini & Hochberg 1995](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x)).
6. **Standardized Climatological Anomalies ([`analyzeTrend.ts`](src/lib/stats/analyzeTrend.ts)):**
   - Flags extreme years ($\|z_i\| > 1.5$) and drives the *Show Your Stripes* visualization ([`<ClimateStripes />`](src/components/share/ClimateStripes.tsx)).

### 3-Tier Offline-First Resilience

Designed so judges and field users never hit a blank screen—even under hackathon Wi-Fi congestion or external API rate limits:

1. **Tier 1 — Instant Zero-Latency Presets & Browser Cache ([`clientInvestigate.ts`](src/lib/nasa/clientInvestigate.ts)):**
   - The `4` featured dossiers (`Case #01–04` inside the `20`-case catalog in [`src/data/investigations.ts`](src/data/investigations.ts)) are bundled for **0 ms offline loading**. Custom live queries and 4-variable scans are cached in memory and browser `localStorage`.
2. **Tier 2 — Cloudflare Workers Edge API + D1 SQLite Cache ([`src/worker/index.ts`](src/worker/index.ts)):**
   - Endpoints: `/api/health`, `/api/investigate`, `/api/investigations` (paginated), `/api/geocode`, `/api/dossiers` (paginated) & `/api/dossiers/:id`, plus `/api/ai/translate` & `/api/ai/interpret`.
   - Backed by **Cloudflare D1 (`earthtrend-db`)** via idempotent auto-initialized tables ([`d1/schema.sql`](d1/schema.sql), [`dbSchema.ts`](src/worker/dbSchema.ts)): `nasa_cache`, `dossiers`, `ai_cache`, and `ai_model_cooldowns`.
3. **Tier 3 — Direct Client Fallback & Deterministic AI Fallback:**
   - If Worker telemetry is unreachable in a static preview environment, `runClientInvestigation()` fetches directly from `https://power.larc.nasa.gov` and runs the TypeScript engine in the browser; if upstream AI is unconfigured or rate-limited, [`fallback.ts`](src/lib/ai/fallback.ts) preserves full functionality locally without failing the user flow.
