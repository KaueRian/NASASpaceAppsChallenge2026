## 1. Case #01 — Vilhena, RO, Brazil (`PRECTOTCORR`, `Apr–Sep`)
- **Hook (EN):** *"Is Vilhena's dry season losing critical rainfall?"*
- **Hook (PT):** *"A estação seca de Vilhena está perdendo chuvas críticas?"*
- **Real Statistical Evidence:** Between 1981 and 2025, cumulative dry-season precipitation (`Apr–Sep`) in Vilhena declined by **`-234.46 mm`** (`-53.29 mm/decade`, Mann-Kendall $S = -379, Z = -3.82, p = 0.000131$). *(Note: full-year `Annual` precipitation in Vilhena fell by `-864.73 mm`, $Z = -5.14, p < 0.000001$, and dry-season mean temperature `T2M` rose `+3.23 °C`, $Z = +5.95$).*
- **Regional Impact & *Missão Amazônia* Alignment:**
  - **Agriculture (Cone Sul of Rondônia):** Vilhena sits on the Chapada dos Parecis at the transition between the Cerrado and the Amazon biome. Losing ~53 mm of dry-season rain per decade directly shortens the planting window for second-crop (*safrinha*) maize and soybeans.
  - **Water & Fire Risk (UN SDGs 6, 13 & 15):** Prolonged dry-season deficits lower headwater streamflows and dry out surface litter, increasing wildfire vulnerability.
  - **Complementary Land-Cover Context:** Pairing this 45-year NASA POWER series with *MapBiomas* land-use transitions helps local schools, researchers, and municipal planners evaluate regional climate-forest interactions while respecting the scientific distinction between **statistical trend significance and single-cause physical attribution**.

## 2. Case #02 — Ariquemes, RO, Brazil (`T2M_MIN`, `Annual`)
- **Hook (EN):** *"Are tropical nights in Rondônia getting persistently hotter?"*
- **Hook (PT):** *"As noites tropicais em Rondônia estão ficando persistentemente mais quentes?"*
- **Real Statistical Evidence:** Annual minimum night-time temperature (`T2M_MIN` at 2 meters) in Ariquemes rose **`+1.82 °C`** (`+0.41 °C/decade`, Mann-Kendall $S = +502, Z = +4.90, p = 0.000001, R^2 = 0.451$).
- **Regional Impact:**
  - **Public Health & Labor:** Elevated nocturnal temperatures prevent buildings and the human body from cooling overnight, increasing cardiovascular and heat-exhaustion risks for rural and urban workers in the Jamari Valley.
  - **Tropical Perennial Crops & Livestock:** Robusto coffee (*Coffea canephora*), cocoa, and dairy cattle in central Rondônia are highly sensitive to night-time minimum temperatures above physiological thresholds, reducing flowering efficiency and milk yield.

## 3. Case #03 — Corumbá (Pantanal), MS, Brazil (`GWETROOT`, `Annual`)
- **Hook (EN):** *"Is the Pantanal biome losing root-zone soil moisture?"*
- **Hook (PT):** *"O bioma Pantanal está perdendo umidade na zona radicular do solo?"*
- **Real Statistical Evidence:** Annual root-zone soil wetness index (`GWETROOT`, 0–100%) in Corumbá declined by **`-5.60 percentage points`** (`-1.27 pts/decade`, Mann-Kendall $S = -434, Z = -4.23, p = 0.000024$; accompanied by a `-534.42 mm` drop in annual rainfall, $p = 0.000003$).
- **National Impact (Brazil):**
  - **Wetland Hydrology & Fire Regimes (UN SDGs 6 & 15):** As the world's largest tropical wetland, the Pantanal depends on multi-month flood pulses from the Upper Paraguay Basin. Persistent loss of root-zone soil moisture exposes organic peat soils and gallery forests to catastrophic wildfire seasons and disrupts Paraguay River navigation.

## 4. Case #04 — Svalbard, Arctic (`T2M`, `Annual`)
- **Hook (EN):** *"How fast is polar amplification warming the High Arctic?"*
- **Hook (PT):** *"Qual é a velocidade real da amplificação polar no Ártico?"*
- **Real Statistical Evidence:** Annual mean air temperature (`T2M`) in Svalbard surged **`+3.50 °C`** (`+0.79 °C/decade`, Mann-Kendall $S = +545, Z = +5.32, p < 0.000001, R^2 = 0.522$).
- **Global Benchmark Impact (UN SDG 13):**
  - **Polar Amplification & Regional Contrast:** Comparing Svalbard (`+0.79 °C/decade`) against tropical Brazil (`+0.41` to `+0.49 °C/decade`) directly demonstrates the HUBEE Challenge Manual principle that **global climate processes drive different warming rates and hydrological responses across distinct latitudes**.
